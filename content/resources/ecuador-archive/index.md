This archive grew out of my own research on Indigenous politics in Ecuador. Over the years, I collected documents that were often difficult to locate, scattered across different repositories, or no longer readily available online. I hope that making these materials easier to discover will support students and researchers working on Ecuador, Indigenous politics, political parties, and social movements.

The catalogue below is publicly available. Access to the documents themselves is granted upon request for academic and educational purposes.
